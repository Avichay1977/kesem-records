Kesem Records / DoGBeaT

I run Kesem Records as my music production studio and home for selected releases. I make music as DoGBeaT, and the studio brings together my productions, collaborations and catalogue.

Avichai Tuchman / DoGBeaT

I'm Avichai Tuchman, also credited as Avi Tuchman or Avichay Tuchman. I make music as DoGBeaT. Based in Tel Aviv, I work as a music producer, composer, bassist and mixing engineer. My sound connects live instruments, rhythmic grooves and electronic textures.

I've worked with Noam Rotem, Reines Girls, Roei Freilich, Balkan Beat Box and Shuli Rand, in different roles on different recordings. My collaborations with Noa Faran includes the six-track EP 'Mikre Avud' (2026). See verified, role-specific credits for the precise contribution.

I play double bass in Oy Division, an Israeli klezmer ensemble. Recordings with the group include Oy Division!, The Unternationale and Dicunt. I also work on music for screen and theater, including projects alongside Assaf Talmudi. Explore music for film, TV and advertising. Rights and licensing routes depend on the recording and version.

If you'd like me to compose, produce or mix your music, start with the DoGBeaT project brief.

What I Do

I produce, compose, arrange, design sound and mix music for artists and screen projects. Some of my recordings can be licensed through their specific Artlist pages; other uses require confirmation from the relevant rights holders. I check the exact recording and credited role before making any licensing claim.

Approach

I aim for precision without sterility. Each sound should have a purpose, and silence matters too. My process combines the feel of live instruments and analog character with the detail and clarity of modern digital production.
