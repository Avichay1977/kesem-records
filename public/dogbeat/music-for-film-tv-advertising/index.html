Music licensing — practical questions

How do I license a track already on Artlist?

Open the song-specific Artlist link and check the correct version and license for your intended project. The link itself does not grant a license.

Can I use music I heard on Spotify or YouTube?

Streaming access is not synchronization clearance. Ask about the specific master recording, publishing rights, territory, media and term before using it.

What if I need an original track?

Use the custom music inquiry. Include a reference, length, edit, deadline and usage. The creative brief and rights are agreed for that project.

For music supervisors, advertising agencies and filmmakers

I can help match my existing DoGBeaT recordings to a film, TV, commercial or branded-content brief, subject to confirmation of the exact recording and the rights holder. For an original brief, I compose and produce music to fit the edit and agree on the deliverables for that project.

Send the intended media, approximate duration, a reference track, a deadline, territory and planned usage. You can start with my original music or my remixes. Listening on Spotify or YouTube is not sync licensing clearance; the appropriate permissions must be obtained separately.

Need help choosing?

Send the project type, reference tracks, deadline and intended use. For existing recordings, include the exact title and version.
