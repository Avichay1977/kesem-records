FREUD DELAY

Prototype · Work in progress · Not for sale

An audio delay experiment exploring rhythm, memory and evolving echoes. The prototype code includes tempo sync, feedback, wet/dry mix, drive, tone, diffusion, modulation and exploratory mood-based controls.

Freud Delay in the promotional video

Shown here is a frame from a Freud Delay promotional video shared in November 2026. It depicts the plug-in interface featured in the video; the project remains a prototype in development.

Earlier concept artwork

Time & tempo

Delay-time control and beat-synced rhythmic divisions for echoes that relate to the track.

Feedback & tone

Repeat density and tonal character; shaping how echoes decay or build into a texture.

Experimental controls

Modulation, diffusion, stereo movement and mood-inspired parameters are under development.

Development status
