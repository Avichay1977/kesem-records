TERROR DELAY

Prototype · Work in progress · Not for sale

A creative delay effect exploring repetition, feedback and rough-edged textures. The prototype source includes time, feedback and mix controls, with tempo sync and drive/saturation experiments.

Terror Delay in the music workstation

The screenshot supplied by DoGBeaT shows the Terror Delay prototype interface open inside a music workstation. Visible controls include feedback, time, mix, depth, rate, spread and a separate Terror control. Its preset menu displays Guerrilla Echo, Tunnel Reverb, Rocket Blast, Resistance Loop and Desert Drift, alongside Arabic, Hebrew and English interface options. These visible interface elements are examples from the prototype, not confirmation that every feature is complete or released.
