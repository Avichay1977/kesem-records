Producer, composer & mixing engineer

Music production, bass & remixes

I produce, compose and mix music, play electric and double bass, and welcome enquiries about remixes, original music and licensing individual recordings.

My documented production work includes the Mikre Avud single with Noa Faran, the Reines Girls album Nishmor Al Hahaverim, and the Noam Rotem album Barzel Ve'avanim. See more documented credits.

I also perform double bass with Oy Division. Hear selected recordings. Other work includes bass on Hemi Rudner's Baderech Le-Ein Gev and my official Balkan Beat Box remix. Explore my original music, production and mixing and Kesem Records.

Credit and artist identity sources: National Library of Israel — Noam Rotem production credit; Qobuz — Balkan Beat Box remix credit; Lemur Magazine — DoGBeaT and Noa Faran feature (by Or Barnea, credited for graphic design and creative consultation on the duo’s “Mikre Avud” single, and for consultation and support on “Zaz Ba’adama”).
